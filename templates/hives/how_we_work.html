  1. 01
    You

    Tell us the valley, the month and the budget

    Use the three-step form, email{% if wa_base %} or WhatsApp{% endif %}. A rough idea is enough: "Ladakh in July, two of us, Comfort" is a good start. Tell us about children, older travellers and any heart or lung conditions, since they shape the route.

  2. 02
    Within one working day

    A route, an altitude profile and an itemised quote

    Your planner sends a day-by-day route with where you sleep, drive times, the sleeping altitude of every night and a written quote with every line priced. Above 3,000 m we pace the climb by the usual advice of 300 to 500 m of sleeping height a day, with rest days. Change anything, as often as you like: swap a hotel, add a night, mix tiers.

  3. 03
    When you are sure

    A deposit confirms the booking

    Accept the quote in writing and pay the deposit. We then confirm every hotel, car, permit and guide in writing, and send current guidance on permits and roads. The balance is due before departure. The amounts, dates and methods are in our payments policy and booking terms.

  4. 04
    On the road

    Your planner stays on WhatsApp

    The same planner is reachable on WhatsApp while you travel. If a pass closes, a flight moves or someone feels the altitude, we reroute with drivers, guides and homestay families who know the valley.
