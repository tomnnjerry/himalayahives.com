Which permits do I need

Hive by hive: Inner Line Permits, Protected Area Permits, park and conservation fees and entry rules, as we understand them in 2026. Choose your nationality to see the lines that apply to you.

Indian citizens need an Inner Line Permit for Arunachal and some border valleys, and pay fees for parts of Ladakh. Foreign nationals need Protected Area Permits for many border areas, a visa for Nepal and a licensed operator for Bhutan. Rules change often: check current status before you travel.

What to carry

  • Original photo ID and several photocopies. Indian citizens: passport or voter ID is accepted in more places than Aadhaar, and is required for Nepal and Bhutan.
  • Foreign nationals: passport with a valid Indian visa, and copies of both, for every checkpost.
  • Printed permits and fee receipts. Phone signal is patchy at checkposts and officers often want paper.
  • The list of districts or areas on your permit. Going beyond them can mean being turned back.

Check current status before you travel

Permit rules in the Himalaya change with security, season and new online systems, sometimes within a year. These notes reflect our understanding in 2026 and are not legal advice. When you book with us, a planner applies where a registered agent can, and sends the current rules with your quote.
