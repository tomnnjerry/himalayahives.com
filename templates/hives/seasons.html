Which hive, which month

The Himalaya has a season somewhere every month. Kashmir and Ladakh peak in summer while the eastern hives sit under the monsoon; in winter it turns round. Pick the month first, then the hive.

Spring (March to May) and autumn (late September to November) suit most hives. June to September is the time for Ladakh, Spiti and the high passes. December to February suits the lower hills and the snow.
