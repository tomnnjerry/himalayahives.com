How we plan a drive

Slow roads, honest times

  • We drive in daylight and keep long road days to about one in three.
  • We add a spare day to any route that crosses a pass above 4,000 m.
  • Above 3,000 m we pace the climb so sleeping height rises no more than 300–500 m a day. This is not medical advice: talk to your doctor before a high trip.

On a budget

Buses and shared jeeps

State buses and shared jeeps run most valley roads in season. Saver trips use them where they run reliably.
