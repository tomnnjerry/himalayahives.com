How Saver trips stay cheap

Where the money goes, and where it does not

{% icon "bus" %}

State buses and shared jeeps

HRTC, KMOU and Bhutan Post buses, union jeeps and shared taxis on routes where they run daily in season. A seat costs a fraction of a private car.

{% icon "home" %}

Homestays and rest houses

Family homes, hostels and government tourist bungalows. Two meals are usually included. Bathrooms may be shared, and hot water may come by the bucket.

{% icon "calendar" %}

Shoulder months

Late March and April, October into November. Rooms cost less, buses are less full and the weather is usually settled.

{% icon "food" %}

Local food

Dal, rice, thukpa, momos and whatever the house is cooking. It is the cheapest food in each valley, and usually the best.

{% icon "altitude" %}

Same altitude rules

Saver trips keep the spare days and the 300–500 m a day rule above 3,000 m. A cheaper trip is never a faster climb.

{% icon "chat" %}

The same planner

One planner on WhatsApp from the first message to the last bus. If a road closes, we rebook.
